Demand scenarios & optimization actions

Interpret stress signals and structure targeted grid interventions

What predicts grid stress

Weather, load, market, and network signals

The model identifies patterns across historical telemetry and operating conditions. Signals should support system-level planning alongside real-time controls and operator judgment.

  • Weather & temperature

    Heat waves, cold fronts, humidity, and precipitation change cooling and heating demand.

  • Load & calendar patterns

    Time-of-day, weekday, seasonal, and industrial production patterns drive the base load curve.

  • Renewable availability

    Solar, wind, and hydro output variability changes dispatch and reserve requirements.

  • Network & market signals

    Transmission constraints, electricity prices, and distributed energy flows alter available capacity.

Use aggregated telemetry for planning. Review data quality, uncertainty, and operational constraints before acting.
